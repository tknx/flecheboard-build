# flècheboard

A wall-mounted foil-fencing training target you can build yourself: five lit targets, a Raspberry Pi that times every touch, a voice that calls the drills, and a phone that shows the results.

Three drills, chosen with the power button:
- **Random:** one to three targets light up; hit any of them, as fast as you can.
- **Programming:** tap out a pattern with its rhythm, and the board plays it back for you to hit.
- **Simon Says:** the board shows a sequence; hit it back in order. Each round adds one more.

**How to build it: [BUILD.md](BUILD.md)** (parts, tools, the box, the wiring). The 3D-printable button flanges are in [`hardware/flanges/`](hardware/flanges/).
